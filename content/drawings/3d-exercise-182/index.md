## Drawing reading checks

- Compare the section and isometric views to trace the main passage, the three straight branches, and the curved end branch.
- Check the flange outlines, mounting-hole positions, wall thickness, and bend radii against the dimensioned views.

## Check your result

- Check each opening against the drawing: its size, center position, and direction must agree across the available views.
- Inspect the internal space as well as the outside shape. Use any section views to check which walls remain and where the passage opens.
