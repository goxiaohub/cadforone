## Drawing reading checks

- Use the dimensioned profile to establish the taper, top rim, bottom thickness, and rounded lower corner.
- Read the note specifying 20 equally spaced holes per ring and equal hole width and spacing before arranging the openings.

## Check your result

- Check the top and bottom diameters, overall height, and rim thickness against the profile.
- Compare the opening rows and solid lower portion with both the dimensioned and isometric views.
