## Drawing reading checks

- Compare the front view and section M-M to establish the fork opening, upright plate thicknesses, and hollow lower boss.
- Use the enlarged hole detail to distinguish the small through-hole from the larger recess, and locate the six flange holes in the plan view.

## Check your result

- Check the base and boss diameters, overall height, and flange thickness against the dimensioned views.
- Verify the spacing of the upright plates, stepped-hole positions, and chamfers against the section and isometric view.
