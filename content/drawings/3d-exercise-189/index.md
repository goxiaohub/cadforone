## Drawing reading checks

- Compare section A-A with the isometric view to establish the plate thickness, raised bridge, and rounded transitions into the base.
- Use the dimensioned plan view to locate the central opening, fixing holes, and elongated slots; distinguish the different hole diameters.

## Check your result

- Check the bridge height, base outline, top-plate width, and corner radii across the views.
- Inspect the underside clearance and semicircular lower relief, then verify that the holes and slots pass through the intended plates.
