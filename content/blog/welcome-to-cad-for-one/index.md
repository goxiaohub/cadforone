Welcome to **CAD For One**, a place to practice reading engineering drawings and building CAD models.

## Choose a drawing to practice

- [2D CAD exercises](/drawings/2d/): 65 drawings for practicing profiles, circles, arcs, and geometric relationships.
- [3D CAD exercises](/drawings/3d/): 181 dimensioned drawings covering individual parts, section views, and assemblies.

Each exercise has its own page and number. Open the drawing, review the reading checks, and work in your preferred CAD software. The notes help you inspect the drawing and compare your result; they do not claim that every exercise has a tested modeling tutorial.

## Learn about your CAD tools

The [CAD Notes blog](/blog/) covers FreeCAD, SolidCraft, modeling habits, and selected technical topics. New to the library? Start with [FreeCAD for drawing practice](/blog/freecad-for-drawing-practice/) or browse the [CAD glossary](/glossary/).

For image permissions and the site's editorial approach, see [About CAD for One](/about/). Send corrections through the [contact page](/contact/) and include the exercise number or page address.
